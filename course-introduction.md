# Course Introduction

CPELX230 is my Elective 2 in the Computer Engineering program at National University for Term 2, SY 2026-2027. The focus of this elective is Applied Machine Learning and AI Engineering. This part of the course covers Agentic Software Engineering, which is about building software with AI agents that can plan and act toward a goal, and doing it responsibly.

To get started, the course asks us to read about software engineering basics (Sommerville), Scrum, Git, intelligent agents (Russell and Norvig), and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. Together these cover how teams build software, how we track and share code, how agents work, and how to manage the risks that come with them.

I am taking this course with Section COEP07.
